what a massive undertaking this has been... here i go again doing something that'll take hours and probably not even be noticed by anyone

NEW FONT

after several months we are finally saying goodbye to Calibri. it felt a little too boring (not "Times New Roman" boring, but still boring) and it made the whole website look like the instructions to an assignment worth 20% of your grade. i believe the vibes we unleash on this website should be more positive than that

as for the new font, Poppins, i've really liked this font for years (i think i have some sort of personal issue with double-storey lowercase A's, so it pleases me that this font doesn't use them). i've never been able to use it in any sort of project because of how goofy it looks... which naturally made it a perfect choice for this website; furthermore, i found out how to use it in a way that doesn't require me to download a million files and/or memorize a lot of special code

THIS PAGE

if you never saw this page on any day that is before today, you missed out on a truly wonderful organizational system. before today, i had a separate page for every single note (like "dr-potter.github.io/notes-2026-09-14.html") as well as 1 master page containing all of the notes. the individual pages do not exist anymore because they were stupid! i should've deleted them as soon as i made the master page; if you have the master page, why would you ever go through the misery of clicking all those individual links? and if you do want to see a specific note, ctrl+f still exists (or you can just scroll down until you find it, the old-fashioned way). so yeah now it's one note page, but as a reminder of what we lost, i continue to use sky blue for this page's background... i do like this color

oh yeah i didn't mention the table. this probably took the longest; i don't really know how you're supposed to understand html tables if you don't have tutorials in front of you. i experimented with so many different layouts and i reformatted both the date text and the body text so many times, and then eventually i used none of those alternate versions and went with essentially the same formatting as before. we are increasing the use of color though, and we're adding solid borders between the notes. i don't know why the whole page was in one color before; it almost feels like i tried my hardest not to make it interesting. regardless, i've now made it cleaner and more eye-catching, and i expect to win some variety of cleanliness and eye-catching-ness award because of my revolutionary work in this field
