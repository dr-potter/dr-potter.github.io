NEW FONT

after several months we are finally saying goodbye to Calibri. it felt a little too boring (not "Times New Roman" boring, but still boring) and it made the whole website look like the instructions to an assignment worth 20% of your grade. i believe the vibes we unleash on this website should be more positive than that

THIS PAGE

if you never saw this page on any day that is before today, you missed out on a truly wonderful organizational system. before today, i had a separate page for every single note (like "dr-potter.github.io/notes-2026-09-14.html") as well as 1 master page containing all of the notes. the individual pages do not exist anymore because they were stupid! i should've deleted them as soon as i made the master page; if you have the master page, why would you ever go through the misery of clicking all those individual links? and if you do want to see a specific note, ctrl+f still exists (or you can just scroll down until you find it, the old-fashioned way). so yeah now it's one note page, but as a reminder of what we lost, i continue to use sky blue for this page's title... i do like this color

oh yeah i didn't mention the table. this probably took the longest; i don't really know how you're supposed to understand html tables if you don't have tutorials in front of you. i experimented with so many different layouts and i reformatted both the date text and the body text so many times. we are increasing the use of color and we're adding solid borders between the notes. i don't know why the whole page was in one color before; it almost feels like i tried my hardest not to make it interesting. regardless, i've now made it cleaner and more eye-catching, and i expect to win some variety of cleanliness and eye-catching-ness award because of my revolutionary work in this field
